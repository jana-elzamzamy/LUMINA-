How it works

  1. 1
    Hand detection. TensorFlow.js loads the Handpose model, which finds 21 landmarks on your hand (knuckles and fingertips) on every video frame.
  2. 2
    Finger state. Lumina checks which fingers are extended vs. curled by comparing landmark positions.
  3. 3
    Gesture + speech. The pattern of extended fingers maps to a sign, which is shown on screen and read out loud.
