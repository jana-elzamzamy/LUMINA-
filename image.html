How it works

  1. 1
    You give an image. Either upload a file from your device or open the camera and capture a photo.
  2. 2
    Lumina analyzes it. A TensorFlow.js object detection model (COCO-SSD) runs on your device and finds objects, their position, and confidence.
  3. 3
    Lumina describes it. The results are turned into a natural sentence ("I can see two people and a dog in the center…") and read aloud through the Web Speech API.
